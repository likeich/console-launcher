Logos are drawn white, trimmed, as the icons draw them. Platforms the set has no logo for take theirs from:
- es-theme-carbon (github.com/fabricecaruso/es-theme-carbon): arduboy, cannonball, cavestory, doom, flash, flashback, j2me, lowresnx, moonlight, palm, ports, quake, quake2, uzebox, vc4000, wasm4
- es-theme-art-book-next (github.com/anthonycaccese/art-book-next-es-de): white versions of arduboy, doom, flash, j2me, lowresnx, palm, ports, quake
- Wikimedia Commons: elektor (File:Elektor-Logo.svg), rpgmaker (File:Program-logo-rpg-maker-2003en.png)
- English Wikipedia: idtech (File:Id_Software.svg)
- Console Launcher's own mark, a Material Symbols Rounded icon (github.com/google/material-design-icons, Apache-2.0) beside the name in Roboto Bold, where no logo exists: chip8 (memory), ebook (menu_book), videos (play_circle)

Heroes are the icons' backgrounds with no logo, carried out to 16:9.
